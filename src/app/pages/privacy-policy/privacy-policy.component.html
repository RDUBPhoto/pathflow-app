PathFlow privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2, 2026

PathFlow is a workflow and communication platform operated by VistaLumina. This policy explains how PathFlow handles information collected through the public website, SMS opt-in pages, and authenticated application.

Information we collect

We may collect name, email address, mobile phone number, vehicle or service details, account activity, quote and invoice information, appointment details, communication history, and SMS consent records.

How we use information

We use information to operate the PathFlow application, manage service workflows, provide customer support, send transactional account and service updates, manage quotes and invoices, and maintain security and compliance records.

SMS messaging and consent

If you provide a mobile phone number and opt in, PathFlow may send transactional SMS messages such as verification codes, appointment reminders, service/job updates, quote and invoice notifications, billing alerts, account alerts, and support responses. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

SMS consent is not a condition of purchase. We store consent method, consent text, consent version, opt-in source, phone number, and timestamps for compliance and audit purposes.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or SMS consent data. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate PathFlow, deliver communications, process payments, provide hosting, or comply with legal obligations.

Opt-out and choices

You can opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP. You may also contact the business using PathFlow or PathFlow support to update communication preferences.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to VistaLumina / PathFlow at robert@pathflow-app.com.
