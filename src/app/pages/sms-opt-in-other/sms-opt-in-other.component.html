PathFlow SMS Opt-In Methods

PathFlow, operated by VistaLumina, sends transactional SMS only after a recipient provides a mobile phone number and gives explicit consent. The primary public opt-in method is the website form.

Primary website form opt-in

A subscriber enters their mobile phone number at /sms-opt-in and must check an unchecked SMS consent box before submitting. The consent disclosure appears next to the checkbox at the time the phone number is collected.

Authenticated app opt-in

Existing PathFlow users may also manage SMS preferences inside the authenticated application. The application records consent method, consent text, consent version, and timestamp for audit purposes.

Message categories

Messages are transactional and may include verification codes, appointment reminders, service/job updates, quote and invoice notifications, billing alerts, and support responses.

Opt-out and help

Every SMS program disclosure tells recipients that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and they can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
